// Medical Record Automation

Your paralegal spends hours sorting records per case.
Aikuir does it in minutes.

Every WC case arrives with a flood of records — scanned mail, email attachments, WC-102 packets from opposing counsel. Sorting, classifying, and summarizing them manually is the single biggest time drain in a WC practice. Aikuir eliminates it entirely.

  • Ingests records from any source: scanned mail, email, WC-102 packets
  • AI classifies every document — medical record, Board form, IME, wage docs
  • Extracts and structures each visit: diagnosis, body parts, treatment, work restrictions
  • Generates a chronological timeline with hyperlinks to every source document
  • Output is ready for demand drafting, deposition prep, or case strategy — immediately

Security Architecture

No cloud. No exposure.
No exceptions.

Every other legal AI platform stores your client data on servers you don't control. Aikuir doesn't. Your data never leaves your walls.

HIPAA-Compliant by Architecture

All PHI is stored locally, encrypted at rest and in transit. Full audit logging of every record access. Compliance isn't a certification — it's the design.

No External Attack Surface

The server is not internet-accessible. Ransomware, breaches, and third-party compromises cannot reach your client data — physically or architecturally.

Role-Based Access Control

Attorneys, paralegals, and admins each see exactly what they need. Granular permissions protect sensitive data at every level.

Bar Advertising Compliance Built In

Auto-generated disclaimers on all marketing content. Client anonymization is on by default. Every post is Bar-compliant before it publishes.
